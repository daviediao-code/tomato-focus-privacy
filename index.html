Privacy Policy

Tomato Focus — Pomodoro Focus Timer for Android

In short: Tomato Focus does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Introduction

Tomato Focus ("we", "our", or "the app") is a Pomodoro focus timer application for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

Tomato Focus does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

All data you create in the app — including your tasks, focus session history, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

The app does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is used solely to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Tasks you create
  • Focus session history and statistics
  • App settings (timer duration, theme, notification preferences)

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions We Request

Tomato Focus requests only the permissions necessary for its core timer functionality:

  • Notifications — to alert you when a focus session or break ends
  • Foreground service — to keep the timer running accurately while the app is in the background
  • Vibration — to provide a physical alert when a session ends
  • Internet — required only for processing in-app purchases through Google Play

We do not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or any other sensitive data.

Third-Party Services

In-app purchases: If you choose to purchase a subscription or lifetime licence, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles payment data.

The app does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Data Security

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, its security is protected by your device's own security measures, such as screen lock and encryption. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

Children's Privacy

Tomato Focus is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the app collects no personal data at all, it poses no risk to users of any age in this regard.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You have full control over all app data through your device settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
